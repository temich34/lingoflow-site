LingoFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the LingoFlow application. This policy explains how LingoFlow handles information when you use the mobile application.

Information we process

Account information may include your email address and authentication identifiers when you create or use an account through email, Google, or Apple. LingoFlow also stores learning preferences such as interface language, learning language, selected level, goals and interests.

Learning progress may include XP, streak information, completed lessons and quizzes, course progress, vocabulary status, spaced-repetition review state, and related learning statistics.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, provide and synchronize learning progress across devices, personalize the learning experience, maintain account security, and provide requested app functionality.

Service providers

LingoFlow uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend functions. Google and Apple may process information when you choose their sign-in services.

Data retention and deletion

Account data and associated cloud learning data are retained while your account is active, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You can initiate permanent account deletion inside LingoFlow from Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also request deletion at https://temich34.github.io/lingoflow-site/delete-account.html.

When an account is deleted, the authentication account and associated LingoFlow profile and cloud progress are deleted, except information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other lawful obligations.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

LingoFlow is intended for users who meet the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

LingoFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the LingoFlow application and explains how information is handled when you use LingoFlow.

Information we process

Account information may include your email address and authentication identifiers when you create or use an account through email, Google, or Apple. LingoFlow also stores learning preferences such as interface language, learning language, selected level, goals and interests.

Learning progress may include streak information, completed lessons and quizzes, course progress, vocabulary status, spaced-repetition review state, saved lessons, and related learning statistics.

Advertising and consent

LingoFlow uses Google AdMob to display banner and native advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other regions where required, LingoFlow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent before requesting ads. Where available, users can review or change their advertising privacy choices from the app's privacy settings.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide and synchronize learning progress across devices, personalize the learning experience, maintain account security, provide requested app functionality, display advertisements, measure advertising performance, and help prevent fraud and abuse.

Service providers

LingoFlow uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend functions. Google may process information through Google Sign-In, Google AdMob, and the Google User Messaging Platform. Apple may process information when you choose Apple sign-in services.

Data retention and deletion

Account data and associated cloud learning data are retained while your account is active, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You can initiate permanent account deletion inside LingoFlow from Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also request deletion at https://temich34.github.io/lingoflow-site/delete-account.html.

When an account is deleted, the authentication account and associated LingoFlow profile and cloud progress are deleted, except information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other lawful obligations. Data processed independently by third-party service providers may be subject to their own retention policies.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Data transmitted by our service providers is protected in transit using appropriate security measures. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

LingoFlow is intended for users who meet the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also be able to change advertising consent choices through the app's privacy settings. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

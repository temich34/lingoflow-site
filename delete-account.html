Delete your LingoFlow account

LingoFlow lets you permanently delete your account and associated cloud learning data.

Delete inside the app

Open LingoFlow → Profile → Account → Delete account, then confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion outside the app

If you cannot access the app, send a request from the email address associated with your LingoFlow account.

Email account-deletion request

Email: support.lingoflow@gmail.com

We may need to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

What is deleted

Deletion is permanent.

The LingoFlow authentication account, profile information, and associated cloud learning progress are deleted. Information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other lawful obligations may be retained only as required.
